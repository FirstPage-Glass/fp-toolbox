Example Finance Group, a HK lending platform, had flat organic growth despite heavy content output.

- Technical audit found 40% of pages unindexable (duplicate meta, soft 404s).

- Launched a weekly programmatic landing page pipeline for product pages.

## Result
- Organic leads up 180% within 6 months.
- Cost per lead from organic down 45% vs paid benchmark.
- Core Web Vitals passed on 95% of pages after the technical cleanup.

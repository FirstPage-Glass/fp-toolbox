client: Harbour Retail Group
industry: Retail & E-commerce
result: 3.2x return on ad spend within one quarter

Harbour Retail Group ran 12 SKUs across Google and Meta with no cross-channel attribution.

## What we did
- Unified tracking across Google Ads, Meta, and GA4.
- Shifted budget toward branded + non-branded search combos.
- Built creative testing loops, retiring underperforming ads weekly.

## Result
- ROAS up from 1.1x to 3.2x within one quarter.
- Non-branded search revenue share grew from 18% to 41%.

# First Page Digital — Brand Guide

First Page Digital is a Hong Kong performance marketing agency with strong APAC presence, serving over 6,000 clients globally.

## Positioning
- Performance-first digital marketing agency.
- Measurable ROI on every channel we run.
- Deep expertise in SEO, paid media, and content.

## Tone
- Direct, opinionated, data-backed.
- We name problems and fix them; we do not sugarcoat.
- Speak in outcomes (revenue, leads, rankings), not activities.

## Proof style
- Cite real client results from the case-study library only. Never fabricate benchmarks.
- Where a number is an estimate, label it as such.

## Channels we sell
- SEO (organic), Google Ads (paid search), Meta (paid social), content marketing, and China platforms (Baidu, WeChat, Xiaohongshu) where relevant — flag ICP licensing requirements for China/GBA.
